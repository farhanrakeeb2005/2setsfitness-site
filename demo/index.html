Try the app

The real app, in your browser

Everything here runs on the same code as the phone: the 3D coach, the day themes, the injury guide and the ranks. The data is a sample, so tap anything. Nothing is saved.

  1. Pick a day in This week: Home changes colour and pose with it.
  2. Start quest and complete sets: the 3D coach shows every rep.
  3. Muscle Map: spin the body, see your week in heat.
  4. Injury guide: tap where it hurts, get a plan and watch the exercises.
