Questions

Quick answers

Does the camera record me?

No. The form analysis runs on your phone while you train. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Can I use it for sport, not only the gym?

Yes. Add football, boxing, swimming and other sports to your week, log them with minutes and effort, and they feed your points and your fighter's stats.

Is the pain guide medical advice?

No. It's general self-care guidance with clear warning signs, and it tells you when to see a doctor or physio.
