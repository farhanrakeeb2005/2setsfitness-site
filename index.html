On your phone

Put it on your home screen.

No App Store needed. Add it once and it sits with your other apps, with its own icon, and opens full screen.

iPhone and iPad

In Safari

  1. Open the app in Safari. It has to be Safari; other browsers on iPhone cannot add it.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow coming out of the top.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, top right. The icon appears with your other apps.
Android

In Chrome

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see that instead. They do the same thing.
  4. Tap Install. The icon appears with your other apps.

On Android you can also ask for the Play version, which adds step counting, sleep tracking and alarms.

Signed in, your training follows you: the same account on the phone, the laptop and the Android app. Nothing to set up twice.

Questions

The short, honest answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. 2SetsFitness is free to download and use. No card needed.

Where can I get it?

Use it in your browser right now, free, on any phone or computer, with nothing to install. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app — that is how it works on an iPhone, since there is no App Store version yet. The Android app is in early access while Google reviews it for public release: ask us for an invite and we will add you.

What is different in the browser?

It is the same account either way: your training, your plan, your coach and your points follow you between them. Five things need the Android app, because a browser cannot do them: Form Coach on your camera, counting your steps, tracking your sleep overnight, alarms, and reminders. Everything else is the same.

Does the camera record me?

No. Form Coach counts and checks your reps on your phone, and the video never leaves it.

Can I bring my workouts from another app?

Yes. Import a Strong, Hevy or FitNotes export, or paste workouts from your notes, and your routines come with them. You can export everything as CSV too.

How do points work?

Workouts, sport sessions, stretches and a good night's sleep all earn points toward your next rank, harder sessions more. Up to 500 a day, so nobody can farm the leaderboard.

How do challenges work?

Add a friend by code, pick a target like 100 push-ups or 10 km, and race. The winner takes the points. It runs on trust: the only person you cheat is yourself.

What happens to my data?

Your workouts are saved to a private space only your account can read, so they come back on a new phone. We don't sell your data, show ads or use ad trackers, and Form Coach's camera video never leaves your phone. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Which languages does it speak?

25, from English and Arabic to Urdu and Turkish.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. No app? Here's how to ask us.
