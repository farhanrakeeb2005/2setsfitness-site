Questions.

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. 2SetsFitness is free to download and use. No card needed.

Where can I get it?

Android first, on Google Play very soon. iPhone is coming later. Until then, try the demo in your browser.

Does Form Coach record me?

No. The camera counts and checks your reps on your phone, and the video never leaves it.

How do points work?

Workouts, sport sessions and a good night's sleep all earn points toward your next rank. Sleeping 7 to 9 hours earns the most.

How do challenges work?

Add a friend by code, pick a target like 100 push-ups or 10 km, and race. The winner takes the points. It runs on trust: the only person you cheat is yourself.

What happens to my data?

Your training stays on your phone unless you make an account to back it up. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Which languages does it speak?

25, from English and Arabic to Urdu and Turkish.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete account. No app? Here's how to ask us.
