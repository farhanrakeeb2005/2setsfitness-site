Delete your 2SetsFitness account

You can delete your 2SetsFitness account and its data at any time, in two ways.

In the app (straight away)

  1. Open 2SetsFitness and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account, your cloud backup, our server's copy of your data (profile, points, leaderboard entry and picture, AI request records) and the data on that phone, straight away.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email trrfarhan@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my 2SetsFitness account". We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their normal schedules. Data stored only on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset everything.
