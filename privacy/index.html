2SetsFitness Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Applies to the 2SetsFitness app for Android and iOS and its services · See also our Terms and Conditions

The short version. Most of your data stays on your phone. If you create an account, your training data is backed up to your own private space in our cloud so you can restore it on a new phone. Camera form analysis runs on your phone and the video is never uploaded. Meal photos and coaching questions are sent to our AI provider only to answer you. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

2SetsFitness ("we", "us") is run by Farhan Rakeeb. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at trrfarhan@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

2.1 Data that stays on your phone

The app is local-first. The following is stored in the app's own storage on your device and is used to run the app:

  • Your profile and settings: name, goal, experience, training days, equipment, gender, age, height, weight, units, language, avatar choices.
  • Your training: routines, weekly schedule, workouts, sets, reps and weights, personal records, form scores, points and ranks.
  • Health and wellbeing logs you enter: body weight and measurements, food and water, recovery and soreness, sports sessions, pain and injury logs.
  • Sleep tracking, if you turn it on (see 2.6).
  • Progress photos you take in the app. These stay on your phone only. They are not backed up or uploaded.

If you use the app without an account, this data never leaves your device (except for the features in 2.4 and 2.5 when you use them). On Android, if you have turned on Google's device backup, the app's stored data may be included in that backup, which Google keeps in your own Google account. You can turn this off in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Backup).

2.2 Your account

If you create an account we collect your email address and the name you give and, if you choose Google sign-in, your Google account name, email and profile ID. If you choose to verify a phone number, we collect that number, and Firebase sends a one-time code to it by SMS. We use these to sign you in, keep your data separate from other users, and send account emails. Sign-in is provided by Google Firebase Authentication.

2.3 Cloud backup (account holders)

When you are signed in, the app backs up the data in 2.1 (except progress photos and sleep recordings) to Google Cloud Firestore, in a private area only your account can read. This lets you restore your data after a reinstall or on a new phone. Your profile, weekly schedule, points and logged nutrition, recovery and sports sessions are also stored by our server (see section 4).

2.4 AI features

When you use the AI coach, workout tips, plans, summaries, advice, sports plans or meal scan, the app sends the information needed to answer to our server, which passes it to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude). This can include:

  • The exercise, sets, reps, weights and form score, plus a short summary of what you trained today and in the last few days.
  • Your question and relevant parts of your profile (for example goal, level, training days, equipment and any injuries you entered).
  • A meal photo, reduced in size on your phone before it is sent, with any note you add about food you already ate, and foods you type in to be estimated. We use them only to estimate the food and nutrition and do not store the photo or the text on our server.

Anthropic processes this to produce the reply under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this data. Do not include information in a question that you do not want processed this way. We keep a record that an AI request was made (your account ID, the feature and the time) to apply fair-use limits. We do not keep the content of the request in that record.

2.5 Barcode lookups

When you scan a food barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. No account details are sent with it.

2.6 Camera, microphone, motion and sleep

  • Camera (Form Coach): your exercise video is analysed on your phone by an on-device pose model. The video is not recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • Camera and photo library (meal scan, progress photos): used only when you take or choose a photo.
  • Microphone: the permission is requested by camera and audio components the app uses. The app does not record audio.
  • Motion sensors and sleep: if you turn on sleep tracking, the phone's accelerometer and screen activity are read during the night to estimate sleep stages. This stays on your phone.
  • Health Connect (Android): if you allow it, the app reads sleep sessions from Health Connect to show your sleep. It does not write to Health Connect, and this data is not used for advertising or shared with third parties.

2.7 Leaderboards

When you are signed in, the app sends your points total to our server, and your name, points and rank appear on the in-app leaderboard, where other signed-in users can see them. Your points are worked out on your phone from what you log; our server limits how fast a total can grow, to keep the leaderboard fair.

Other signed-in users can also tap your name to open your player profile. It shows your name, your leaderboard picture (your emoji, or a small copy of your profile photo if you set one), your points, rank and place, the month you joined, how your fighter looks (its colours, hairstyle and outfit), and a summary your phone works out from your logs: how many workouts and sport sessions you have logged, your current and best streak, how many personal records you have, and your three most-logged sports (with sessions and hours) and exercises (with how many workouts they were in). It does not show your logs themselves, your weights, body measurements, photos, meals, email or anything else.

If you don't want to be seen, turn off Settings → Data & privacy → Show me on the leaderboard: you are then left off the leaderboard and your profile can't be opened by anyone else. You can also use a nickname as your name, or use the app without an account.

2.8 Challenges and friends

You can challenge another signed-in player from their profile. Our server stores the challenge (who sent it, who it is for, what it is, how many days, any points or forfeit on it) and works out the result. For a target or routine challenge it reads, from your backed-up workouts and sports sessions, only the reps, distances and routine names logged during the challenge. Your opponent sees your progress toward the goal, not your logs.

You can add friends by their friend code or by searching their name (only players shown on the leaderboard can be found). We store who has asked whom and who is friends; your friends see your name, picture, rank and points, and where you are on the rank journey map. You can remove a friend at any time. You can also block any player: no challenges or friend requests between you, either way, and you stop being friends. Blocked players are listed in Settings, where you can unblock them.

2.9 Notifications and alarms

Workout reminders, streak reminders (for example "Don't break your 20-day streak"), the evening fuel check, rest timers and alarms are scheduled on your phone from your own data. Nothing is sent to us to schedule them. Challenge notifications (for example "Sam challenged you") are sent by our server through Firebase Cloud Messaging: for that we store your phone's push token and app language with your account, and delete the token when you sign out. We do not use push notifications for tracking or advertising. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

2.10 Emails

Account holders may receive a welcome email and occasional app update emails. Every update email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

2.11 What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use your health data for marketing.

3. Health data

Some of what you log (body measurements, food, sleep, recovery, pain and injuries) is health data. We process it only to provide the features you use, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give when you enter it or turn the feature on. You can stop at any time by deleting the entries, turning the feature off, or deleting your account. 2SetsFitness is a fitness and wellbeing app, not a medical device. Its guidance, including the pain and injury guide, is general information and not medical advice.

5. International transfers

Some providers (for example Google, Anthropic and Resend) may process data outside the UK and EEA, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it, use "Reset everything" in Settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Account, backup and server data are kept while you have an account. Delete account in the app removes them straight away; if you email us instead, we delete them within 30 days. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their normal schedules.
  • AI request records (account ID, feature, time) are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Meal photos are not stored on our server.
  • Finished challenges are shown for 14 days and kept while you have an account.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR and California law) you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time. To do this, email trrfarhan@gmail.com from your account email address. We will reply within one month.

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account, or email us. This deletes your account, your cloud backup, our server's copy of your data and the data on that phone.
  • Delete data on your phone: Settings → Reset everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Permissions: you can turn the camera, photos, notifications, motion and Health Connect access on or off at any time in your phone's settings.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Cloud backup is protected by Firebase security rules so only your account can read it, and our server checks your sign-in on every request. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and limit who can access it.

9. Children

2SetsFitness is not intended for children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK where that age applies to consent for online services. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
